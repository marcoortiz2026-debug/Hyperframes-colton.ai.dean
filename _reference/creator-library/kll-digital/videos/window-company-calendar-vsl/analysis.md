## Hook (0–2s)
- 0.0–0.2s: the speaker starts punched in and motion-blurred, and the frame settles to a medium shot. It's a zoom-out "arrival" with no title card, so the speaker is talking from frame 1.
- 0.2–0.5s: a 3D glass icon card (a white window icon on a dark rounded tile) tilts in at lower centre. Simultaneously the first qualifier phrase builds word by word from the left in **gold ALL-CAPS with a soft glow**.
- 1.0–2.5s: the phrase clears and the second qualifier (the revenue threshold) builds in the same gold.
- 3.0–4.5s: the pain phrase comes in **red with a red glow** and its own icon card (a calendar). Color carries meaning: gold = who you are, red = the problem.
- Structure: call out the buyer and their size, name the pain, promise the fix and the timeframe. Captions exist *only* in the hook, not for the rest of the video.

## Pacing
- Hard cuts: 15 shots, average 9.5s, median 8.8s (range 1.1–19.9s), about 6 hard cuts/min.
- Visual events are much denser than the cuts suggest: every proof claim gets a full-screen graphic, and talking-head stretches get slow push-ins.
- Rough time split: ~66% talking head (desk, wooden slat wall), ~34% full-screen graphics or B-roll.
- Shape: hook (0–10s) → "insight" B-roll story (18–31s) → credibility (name lower-third, 39–42s) → social proof marquee (53–56s) → three case studies, almost all graphics (57–99s) → offer and CTA, mostly plain talking head (100–143s). **Graphics density peaks in the proof section and drops in the offer section.** The ask is delivered face to face.

## Captions
- Style: **keyword phrase callouts, not full subtitles.** Only the hook (0–5s) and a few B-roll lines are captioned. The talking head is otherwise uncaptioned.
- Font feel: heavy geometric sans, ALL CAPS, tight tracking, an outer glow in the phrase color.
- Entry: words slide in from the left with a short blur trail. The phrase builds word by word in sync with speech, holds, then clears before the next phrase.
- Active-word treatment: none (no karaoke). Emphasis comes from the color of the whole phrase (gold vs red).
- Position: lower third, roughly over the desk, left of or overlapping the icon card.
- Size: medium (~6% of frame height).
- On B-roll: small white ALL-CAPS phrases at frame centre ("at 9 PM", "within sixty seconds") that echo the spoken phrase.

## Transitions
- Flavors observed: hard cut (the default; talking head → graphic and back), swirl/zoom-blur (66s), orange light-leak flash (115s), and a zoom-blur settle on frame 1.
- Rotation: mostly hard cuts. A stylised transition marks a **section change** (proof → next proof, proof → offer). It doesn't run on every cut.
- Signature: hard cut into a black-and-red graphics canvas. Moving between the "real world" (warm wood, speaker) and the "data world" (black, red glow, glass cards) is the rhythm of the whole video.

## Face treatment
- Mode: fullscreen talking head; a split layout once (speaker left, panel right). No picture-in-picture.
- Grading: warm, natural. Warm wood slat wall, navy sweater, white collar; contrast moderate.
- Camera motion: **slow digital push-ins** of about 10–15% over roughly 2s on emphasis lines (measured at 45.0→47.0s), then a hold at the tighter crop. Wide/tight alternation across sections. No handheld shake.
- Framing: centred, desk and laptop in frame, head at the top third.

## Audio reactivity
- Text pulses on beat: no.
- Background reactivity: no.
- Sync is to **speech, not music**: counters land on the spoken number, checklist items appear on each spoken word, and the caption phrase builds with the words.

## Audio (music + SFX)
- Music: a **continuous low bed**, never silent (the quietest 0.5s window is −31.7 dBFS against speech at about −18.5). It's ducked well under the voice and reads as a steady corporate/ambient pulse. No obvious drops.
- SFX: a broadband impact on frame 1 (the hook), and low swells at the 66s swirl and around 80–83s (case-study changes). Whooshes appear tied to the stylised transitions, not to every cut.

## Signature move
Every proof claim becomes a **floating glass stat card on a black/red canvas whose counter rolls up and lands on the number exactly as it's spoken.** The case studies are built as one evolving card system: a counter, then a chart, then a jobs counter, then a before/after pair.

## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- **Stat-card system for case studies.** Build one reusable card sub-composition (name chip + subtitle + counter + optional area chart + optional "jobs" pill), restyled per case study. GSAP: counter tween with `snap` on `innerText`, `ease: "power2.out"`, `duration` timed so the final value lands on the word's timestamp; card in with `rotationY: -12 → -4`, `y: 40 → 0`, `opacity`; slow float `rotation` ±2° yoyo. Map to `data-chart` for the area line if useful.
- **Before/after card pair:** the "WITHOUT US" chart animates first, then "WITH US" slides in beside it and its number overtakes. Strong for HVAC results (missed calls before vs booked jobs after).
- **Hook captions only, in two semantic colors.** Positive/target phrase in the brand accent, pain phrase in warn red, both with a glow and a 3D icon tile. Phrase builds word by word on speech timing and clears between phrases.
- **Slow push-in on emphasis lines** (scale 1.0→1.12 over ~2s on the video wrapper, then hold). Use it instead of jump-zooms in long talking stretches.
- **Triptych B-roll with a focus walk** for a "picture the customer" moment: three panels, a blur filter on two of them, focus moving per clause.
- **Notification-pill marquee** for "we work with N companies" social proof.
- **Icon checklist side panel** (speaker shifts left, items appear per spoken word) for "what you get on the call".
- **Persistent CTA pill** with a down-arrow chip for the last ~6s.
- **Graphics density follows the script section:** dense in proof, sparse in offer and CTA.
- **Stylised transitions only at section boundaries** (`whip-pan` / `swirl-vortex` / `light-leak` registry blocks); hard cuts everywhere else.
